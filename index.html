Linking Ecovacs Robot to Dashboard

Option 1: Direct App Launch
Tap the "Ecovacs App" button on the card to launch the native Ecovacs iOS App via URL scheme.

Option 2: Connect via Home Assistant Integration (Recommended)
You can integrate your Ecovacs Deebot / Winbot into Home Assistant to control cleaning & mopping directly from this screen!

  1. Open your Home Assistant Dashboard.
  2. Go to Settings ➔ Devices & Services.
  3. Click Add Integration ➔ Search for Ecovacs Deebot (or install via HACS).
  4. Log in with your Ecovacs Home account credentials.
  5. Your mopping robot entity (e.g. vacuum.deebot) will now sync status & controls live!
